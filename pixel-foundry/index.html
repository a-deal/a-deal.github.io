05 / Before we start

What working together looks like.

When is Pixel Foundry a good fit?

When an operating problem needs both engineering and product judgment. That could mean connecting equipment data to usable applications, improving a costly manual workflow, integrating an existing system, or deciding which product capability to build next. The strongest fit is a team working in agriculture, industrial operations or defense that wants direct, hands-on help.

What will the engagement deliver?

We agree on specific outputs before work begins. Depending on the problem, those could include a workflow map and prioritized plan, working software and integrations, an architecture your team can extend, evaluation tools and a pilot plan, or a product roadmap and operating model. Success measures, scope and ownership are part of that agreement.

How do we decide whether AI belongs in the solution?

Start with the task, the available data, the cost of mistakes and the people using the system. Compare AI with process changes, existing tools and conventional software. Use a bounded prototype or evaluation to test whether it improves the result enough to justify its complexity and ongoing cost.
